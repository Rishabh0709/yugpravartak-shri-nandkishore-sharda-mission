Our Name, Our Philosophy

What Is Manidweep

The name Manidweep was given by Madhubala Ji Advani — a gesture of reverence toward her spiritual guide, Shri Nandkishore Sharda, whose wisdom continues to shape every initiative carried out in his name.

Every activity of the Mission follows a deliberate, long-term vision — never random, never fragmented. As society's needs have changed, new programmes have been introduced, but the underlying purpose has stayed constant: the holistic development of every individual — education, health, values, spirituality and social responsibility, woven into one balanced whole.

Our Programmatic Scope

Key Activities & Initiatives

Most organisations focus on a single sector — education, healthcare, or food security. Ours is different: rather than a checklist of services, we work toward the holistic development of the whole person, integrating education, value-based learning, health, yoga, meditation, self-reliance, women’s empowerment, and social responsibility into one balanced framework.

🎓

Inclusive Educational Support

From government-school girls to Engineering, Medicine, Pharmacy, CA and MBA students — need-based scholarships accompany deserving students at every stage.

📖

Sunday Value & Mentorship Classes

Weekly sessions led by Madhu Maa and Trust members build character, discipline, positive thinking, and spiritual awareness.

🌾

Dignified Ration Assistance

Begun in October 2020, this programme now supports roughly 300 vulnerable families every month — always with dignity, never as charity.

🧵

Vatsalyamayi Sewing Centre

Established in 2025, offering free tailoring and garment-construction training that builds self-reliance in underprivileged women.

🧘

Four-Dimensional Development

Free daily Yoga, Pranayama, and meditation classes cultivate inner peace, discipline, and resilience for anyone who joins.

🌱

Environmental Stewardship

Tree plantation and responsible use of natural resources are encouraged as a duty toward the generations still to come.
