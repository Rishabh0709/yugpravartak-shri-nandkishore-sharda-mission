A Path for the Present Age

Buddhi-Vivek Yog Sadhna

A practical method that awakens intellect and discernment, refines life and leads toward love, peace and joy.

Context

A Simple Spiritual Path in the Intellectual Age

Bhaiyaji propounded this method for modern life where information is abundant but discernment is needed.

Practice

Spiritual Progress while Living a Material Life

It does not demand rejection of worldly responsibilities; it teaches refinement and balance.

Lotus

Purity like a Lotus

The seeker lives in the world while remaining free from unnecessary attachment.

Six Steps

From Knowledge to Karma-Sadhna

The method unfolds through six stages.

Knowledge

Understanding life and truth.

Science

Inquiry and testing without prejudice.

Reflection

Thoughtful examination of purpose.

Resolve

Firm commitment to the goal.

Surrender

Trustful offering to the Divine.

Karma-Sadhna

Action free from ego and restless desire.

Experiences

Love, Peace and Joy

As Sadhna matures, these experiences flower.

Love

Selfless love connects the seeker with the Divine.

Peace

A balanced and undisturbed state of mind.

Joy

The flowering of Divine connection.

Source

Knowledge Flowing from Siddhpeeth

These principles originated where Bhaiyaji received Maa’s guidance.

Conclusion

Toward Material Progress and Spiritual Ascent

Buddhi-Vivek Yog Sadhna helps a person refine life, awaken discernment and uplift oneself and others through balanced action.

Material Progress with Spiritual Ascent

This Sadhna uplifts oneself and others.
