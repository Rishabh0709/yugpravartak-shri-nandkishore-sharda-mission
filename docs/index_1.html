Genesis & Philosophy

About the Mission

I. Divine Origin & Spiritual Path

Shri Nandkishore Ji Sharda (Bhaiyaji) introduced Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana—a balanced, modern path uniting Wisdom (Gyan), Devotion (Bhakti), and Unselfish Action (Nishkama Karma) under the divine grace of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari.

Read Complete Divine Origin Narrative ↓

Under a special mission, Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari brought Shri Nandkishore Ji Sharda down to Earth to give a simplified spiritual path for the modern scientific age. By establishing Maa as his Guru, Bhaiyaji attained divine wisdom and paved the spiritual path alongside Sushri Basanti Ji Manihar and Sushri Madhubala Ji Advani, founding the "Manidweep Adhyatma Parivar".
