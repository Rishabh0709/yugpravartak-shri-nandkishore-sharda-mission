The Earthly Realm
of Jagatjanani Maa

An Awakening Center of Faith, Spiritual Practice, and Humanity Imbued with the conscious energy and divine grace of Maa Tripurasundari, shaped by the visionary devotion of Shri Nandkishore Sharda, Maa Basanti Manihar, and Maa Madhu Advani, Manidweep brings the spirit of "Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam" to life with selfless service provided under Shri Nandkishore Sharda Gyan Ganga Mission.
