A journey of recognition

Awards & Recognition

From 1999 to today — three generations of selfless education service, recognised by the state government, district administration and social institutions. This honour belongs not to any one person, but to the selfless spirit of service that shaped thousands of students' futures.

1999 – 2003

Bhaiyaji — the Bhamashah honour

For five consecutive years, Rajasthan's Panchayati Raj & Primary Education Department honoured Bhaiyaji as a Bhamashah — a public benefactor — for his contribution of scholarships and furniture to border-area schools of Jodhpur and Barmer districts.

  1. 1999

    Bhamashah honour certificate

    28 June 1999 — for public contribution toward scholarships and furniture for border-area primary/secondary schools of Jodhpur district.

  2. 2000

    Bhamashah honour certificate

    28 June 2000 — the second year's recognition of continuing public service in the education campaign.

  3. 2001

    Bhamashah honour certificate

    28 June 2001 — the third year's recognition of continuing public service in the education campaign.

  4. 2002

    Bhamashah honour certificate

    28 June 2002 — the fourth year's recognition of continuing public service in the education campaign.

  5. 2003

    Bhamashah honour certificate — the fifth and last

    28 June 2003 — this certificate arrived after Bhaiyaji's passing. He always dedicated these honours to the very students for whom the contribution had been made, never treating them as his own.

Roads named in his honour

Municipal authorities in Jodhpur, Balotra and Jasol dedicated major roads to Bhaiyaji's name, giving his work a lasting place in the cities he served.

Jodhpur Nandkishore Sharda Marg 28 April 2012

From Fifth Road Circle to Twelfth Road Circle

Balotra Adhyatmyogi Shri Nandkishore Sharda Marg 16 August 2013

From the first railway crossing to Mansarovar Hospital

Jasol Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda Marg 8 November 2022

From Jasol Laghu Udyog Mandal to Tilwara Circle

2006 – 2017

Maa Basanti — administrative & social honours

After Bhaiyaji's passing, Maa Basanti carried forward both the sadhana and the service — and district administrations and social institutions honoured her again and again for it.

  1. 2006

    Honour certificate

    3 June 2006 — recognition for her contribution to education service.

  2. 2006

    Honour certificate

    4 June 2006 — recognition for her contribution to education service.

  3. 2008

    Honour certificate

    23 December 2008 — social recognition of continuing selfless service.

  4. 2017

    Honour certificate

    15 August 2017 — recognition of decades of unbroken education service.

2023 – 2025

Madhu Maa — recognition of the present leadership

Under Madhu Maa's leadership, district administration, panchayat samitis and women's-achievement institutions have continued to honour the Mission's work — and the recognition continues today.

  1. 2023

    District Bhamashah honour

    Honoured by the district administration as a Bhamashah for her contribution to education service.

  2. 2025

    Panna Dhai honour

    8 March 2025, International Women's Day — honoured for her contribution to social service.

  3. 2025

    Women Achiever honour

    8 March 2025, International Women's Day — for distinguished contribution in the field of women's empowerment.

  4. 2025

    Panchayat Samiti honour, Keru

    Honoured by the Keru Panchayat Samiti for her contribution to regional education service.

  5. 2025

    District Bhamashah honour

    Honoured once again by the district administration in recognition of continuing education service.

Continuity of service, beyond any honour

These certificates and road-namings are not one person's achievement — they are society's and the administration's trust in three unbroken generations of selfless education service.
