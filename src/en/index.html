The Mission's Purpose and Introduction

Under a special mission for human welfare, Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari and Lord Shiva (Babuji) brought their beloved son, Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda (Bhaiyaji), to this earth.

Through complete surrender, true devotion, firm resolve, hard sadhana, tireless effort and selfless love, Bhaiyaji pleased Jagatjanani Maa and received her direct darshan, and taking ‘Maa’ herself as guru, received true, eternal, universal, science-based Divya Gyan. He offered this transcendent knowledge to humanity through his timeless work ‘The Wonderful World After Death’. Bringing together knowledge, devotion, selfless action and yoga sadhana in keeping with the modern scientific age, he set out the ‘Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana’, so that a person could live with joy, peace, happiness and love, and bring about the welfare of their human life.

Under Bhaiyaji's guidance, the spiritual path was opened for many sadhaks, including Sushri Basanti Manihar and Sushri Madhubala Advani (Madhu Maa), and the ‘Manidweep Adhyatm Parivar’ was founded.

At ‘Maa's’ command, Bhaiyaji began public-welfare projects under Nishkam Karma Sadhana — education, sanskar, scholarships and women's empowerment. After he left the physical body, Maa Basanti Manihar, inspired by him, carried forward the publishing and free distribution of literature and many works of human service, all of which Madhu Maa capably runs today. Under her guidance, the ‘Manidweep Adhyatm Parivar’ continues to work with unbroken devotion towards world brotherhood, world peace and human welfare.

{# ============ 1c · THE QUEST (mirrors src/index.html's Seeker section) ============ #}

Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda

Shri Nandkishore Sharda was born on 16 July 1944 in Jodhpur (Rajasthan), into a prosperous and devout family. He was a bright student of science, with a rare mind and a radiant personality.

At only 15, after his father's death, a deep detachment awoke within him before his father's burning pyre.

At that very moment he resolved that he would seek out this truth — what is the power that keeps the physical body moving? Who am I? Where have I come from? What is there after death? Does God exist?

To fulfil this resolve he put questions to many sadhus and saints, tantriks and mahatmas, and read many religious books — but nowhere did he find a satisfying answer.

At last, by the grace of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari, he found a wonderful peace in ‘Maa's’ temple on a tekri behind the Mehrangarh fort in Jodhpur. Becoming a child, he began to call to ‘Maa’ day and night, with selfless love and longing. Hearing his true call, ‘Maa’ could not hold herself back — she gave him her direct darshan and accepted him as her beloved son.

Eternally devoted at the feet of Jagatjanani Maa, a selfless karma-yogi, the even-minded Siddhapurush Yugpravartak Bhaiya Shri Nandkishore Sharda gave a new way of thinking, a new method and new knowledge, suited to the material, social and spiritual needs of this age. He set in motion a new consciousness in this age and awakened it, breathing new life into the spirit of vasudhaiva kutumbakam, world brotherhood, world peace and human welfare, until on the blessed day of Vivekananda Jayanti, 24 January 2003, he left the physical body. Blessed is the earth to have received such a beloved son.

The Loving Maa Basanti Manihar

Born 28 September 1942 in Jodhpur, into a most respected, distinguished and cultured family. Drawn by the knowledge, sadhana and divine personality of her cousin Bhaiyaji, she gave up every comfort and, from 1964, in a small 6×7 foot room in the Manihar building, began the sadhana of Maa Tripurasundari under Bhaiyaji's guidance. Her father put both her and her guide Bhaiyaji through several difficult spiritual tests before giving her his permission to enter the spiritual life.

Under Bhaiyaji's guidance, through complete surrender and childlike selfless love, continuous chanting, austerity and hard sadhana, she received Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari's boundless grace, tenderness and divine darshan. Jagatjanani Maa, too, accepted her as her beloved daughter.

After Bhaiyaji left the physical body, she led all his spiritual and service work to success. Under her guidance, several spiritual works including ‘The Wonderful World After Death’ were published, and the projects of education, sanskar, women's empowerment, scholarships, free literature, food-grain distribution and human service were widened.

She made Bhaiyaji's deep and eternal knowledge simple, practical and accessible, carrying it to countless seekers, and made the ‘Manidweep Adhyatm Parivar’ a powerful means of spiritual awakening under the shade of her own tenderness.

On 7 May 2021, the loving Maa Basanti Manihar left the physical body. Her whole life stood as a living example of love, surrender, tenderness and selfless service, and remains a source of inspiration for countless seekers even today.

The Compassionate Maa Madhubala Advani

Born in 1951 in Sirsa, Punjab, into a family of modern outlook. From the start her personality was formed of independent thought, a reasoned view, fearlessness and firm resolve, alongside integrity and humility. Though her inner self was moved by the true spiritual presence of Bhaiyaji and Maa Basanti, she wished to test her experience against reason, and so took an MA in Psychology. In the end her discernment gave her faith a firm ground, and in 1970, under the loving guidance of Bhaiyaji and Maa Basanti, she accepted the spiritual path in complete surrender.

She absorbed the eternal knowledge given by Bhaiyaji, and through more than five decades of continuous reflection, sadhana, chanting, austerity and service received deep spiritual experiences and the blessings of great conscious powers.

She also carried the important charge of setting down Bhaiyaji's Divya Gyan as the timeless work ‘The Wonderful World After Death’. After Maa Basanti Manihar left the physical body in 2021, she capably took up the leadership of the ‘Manidweep Adhyatm Parivar’.

Today, under her guidance, this spiritual family carries forward the spread of the ‘Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana’ along with education, sanskar, scholarships, women's empowerment, literature, yoga and human service, advancing the purpose of world brotherhood, world peace and human welfare. Madhu Maa's simple, humble and selflessly devoted life is a living channel of this spiritual tradition and a source of inspiration for countless seekers.

Guiding Principles

These principles keep scholarship support from being mere financial aid — they make it a means of education, sanskar, personal development and transparent service.

  • 1
    No one should be denied education

    Financial hardship should never stand in the way of a girl's studies. Education support should be a means towards her academic, moral, intellectual, financial and social advancement.

  • 2
    Education is incomplete without sanskar

    Girls should develop devotion to knowledge, dignity, self-confidence, self-respect, human sensitivity and compassion.

  • 3
    Rights should come with awareness of duty

    Students should understand their duties alongside their rights. Along with an equal right to nature's resources, they should develop love and respect for nature.

  • 4
    Development should be whole and balanced

    A positive coordination of mental, physical, intellectual and spiritual aspects, so that a girl's personality develops in a balanced and empowered way.

  • 5
    Members personally bear administrative costs

    All administrative expenses are borne personally by trust members, so that the trust's entire income can go towards scholarships.

  • 6
    Funds are kept secure, the interest goes to scholarships

    Contributed funds are kept safe as fixed deposits in banks, and the entire interest earned is distributed among the girls as scholarships.
