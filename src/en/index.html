{# ============ 3 · WHAT THE MISSION IS ============ #}
An introduction

What this mission is for

Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda (Bhaiyaji), seeing the need of today's age in the material, social and spiritual spheres alike, gave a new way of thinking, a new method of practice and new knowledge, and began a new spiritual consciousness in this age. For this reason he is called ‘Yugpravartak’ — one who turns the age.

Bringing together knowledge, devotion, selfless action and yoga, he set out the householder-friendly ‘Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana’. Sushri Basanti Manihar joined this path in 1964 and Madhu Maa in 1970; with them, the spiritual path was opened for many sadhaks, and the ‘Manidweep Adhyatm Parivar’ was founded.

In 1996, at ‘Maa's’ command, alongside the spiritual practice Bhaiyaji also began Nishkam Karma Sadhana — education, sanskar, scholarships and human service. Today, under Madhu Maa's leadership, this family carries both forward, steadily — the spread of Divya Gyan, and selfless service.

From self-welfare towards the welfare of the world

Our vision

The founding of a society marked by world brotherhood, world peace and human welfare, where the path to the good of every life is cleared.

Our purpose

Through the Divya Gyan given by Bhaiyaji — resolving a person's spiritual questions — to draw each person, through the ‘Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana’, towards their own welfare by good work and worship.

{# ============ 3 · THE SEEKER (1959) ============ #}
The start of a search · 1959

The resolve of a fifteen-year-old boy

Born on 16 July 1944 into a prosperous and devout family of Jodhpur, Bhaiyaji was a bright student of science with a rare mind. At only 15, after his father's death, a deep detachment awoke within him before his father's burning pyre.

“I will seek out this truth — what is the power that keeps the physical body moving? Who am I? Where have I come from? What is there after death? Does God exist?”

To fulfil this resolve he put questions to many sadhus and saints, tantriks and mahatmas, and read many religious books — but nowhere did he find a satisfying answer.

{# ============ 4 · DISCOVERY & THE BELIEF ============ #}
Divya Gyan

Eternal knowledge, received by taking ‘Maa’ as guru

At last, by the grace of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari, he found a wonderful peace in ‘Maa's’ temple on a tekri behind the Mehrangarh fort in Jodhpur. Becoming a child, he began to call to ‘Maa’ day and night, with selfless love, longing and the ache of separation. Hearing his true call, ‘Maa’ could not hold herself back — she gave him her direct darshan and accepted Bhaiyaji as her beloved son.

Taking ‘Maa’ herself as guru, he received a science-based Divya Gyan and offered it to humanity as the timeless work ‘The Wonderful World After Death’ — a simple, reasoned and science-based account of the origin of the cosmos, the divine realms, brahma-particles, the physical and conscious body, the existence of God and the state after death — for a person of any religion, caste, age and level of learning.

Through an open reading and reflection on this book, many who did not believe in God have undergone a spiritual transformation.

Why is this work distinctive?

Original

Grounded in the Divya Gyan received from Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari.

Scientific

A balanced coming-together of science and spirituality.

Simple

An easy, accessible explanation of deep mysteries.

Eternal

An authoritative guide to life, death, the soul and the cosmos.

Today, through the ‘Shri Nandkishore Sharda Gyan Ganga Mission’, this nectar-like knowledge is carried, free of charge, to everyone, and many scholars have praised it too.

{# ============ 5 · BUDDHI-VIVEK YOGA SADHNA ============ #}
A simple practice for today's age

The Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana

Grounded in eternal knowledge, for the modern age — an art of living.

According to Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda, God has given a human being two priceless gifts — buddhi and vivek. When buddhi becomes pure, vivek awakens through knowledge, and awakened vivek gives a positive direction to a person's thought, character, decisions and life. This is the root purpose of the practice.

To help a person hold in life the eternal Divya Gyan received from Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari — which Bhaiyaji offered to humanity as ‘The Wonderful World After Death’ — and to help them experience joy, peace and inner progress even while living a material life, he set out this simple, scientific and experience-tested practice, which awakens vivek through knowledge and carries a person from the human towards the divine.

How is it distinctive?

Simple
A practical practice that can be taken up easily within a busy modern life.
Scientific
A balanced coming-together of science, reason, experience and spirituality.
Whole
A path of knowledge, devotion, selfless action and yoga — the ground of both material success and inner progress.
Universal
Beyond the bounds of caste, religion, age, class and country, equally relevant for the whole of humanity.

This is not merely a method of practice, but a whole way of living — one that grows in a person positive thought, confidence, self-restraint, discernment and virtue, and makes them a means to the welfare of themselves, their family, society and humanity.

Today this practice is taught at Manidweep and at the Adhyatmyogi Shri Nandkishore Sharda Sadhana-Sthali (Siddhapeeth) — suited to each sadhak's questions, intellectual capacity and personal circumstances. The more the longing and the practice, the more the true happiness and true peace.

{# ============ 6 · CHAKRA DHARAN ============ #}
The divine moment of taking up the chakra

The start of a change of age

“Now I take up the chakra.”

In 1990, during a spiritual journey through South India, when Bhaiyaji and some members of the ‘Maa Parivar’ reached Goa, on a rock amid the sea Bhaiyaji spoke this, calm and grave, and calling on the divine powers took the symbolic posture of taking up the chakra. His luminous face, divine radiance and the awareness visible in him became an unprecedented experience for those present — the whole atmosphere throbbed with joy, peace and divine energy. After resting a few moments in that supernatural state, Bhaiyaji slowly returned to his natural form.

This moment was the highest fruition of Bhaiyaji's long sadhana, the knowledge gained on his spiritual journeys, the divine conscious powers he had received, and the grace of Jagatjanani Maa — Jagatjanani Maa herself had Bhaiyaji take up the chakra. It was not merely a spiritual event, but a divine proclamation of human welfare, the start of a new age, an awakening of thought grounded in buddhi and vivek, and the founding of world brotherhood and world peace.

After the taking up of the chakra, along with the spiritual sadhana of knowledge and devotion, selfless karma-sadhana for public welfare was also joined in, and the change of age began. In this same course, in 1992 Jagatjanani Maa said to Maa Basanti in meditation — “A house has been made for you” — a sign of the building of ‘Manidweep’.

{# ============ 7 · MANIDWEEP ============ #}
A sadhana-sthali built at Maa's command

Manidweep — where to sit is itself sadhana

After Bhaiyaji's taking up of the chakra and Jagatjanani Maa's command, in 1995 this building at A-183, Shastri Nagar was ready. At ‘Maa's’ command it was named ‘Manidweep’ — which, wondrously, is the very name of ‘Maa's’ divine realm. On 10 June 1995 the members of the ‘Maa Parivar’ moved from the Manihar building to Manidweep.

This command came because Jagatjanani Maa herself was to be seated here, guiding the work to come — and enough space was needed for a wide karma-sadhana.

Manidweep is not merely a building.

It is a centre full of faith, sadhana, love, awareness and sanskar, where Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari herself is seated — a sadhana-sthali steeped in conscious particles. Here, simply sitting with a calm mind fills a sadhak with positive thought, and joy begins to be felt of itself; coming again and again awakens within compassion, self-restraint, love and the spirit of ‘vasudhaiva kutumbakam’. All are welcome each day from 10:00 am to 8:00 pm.

Born 28 September 1942, Jodhpur. Bhaiyaji's first cousin, Maa Basanti, drawn by his knowledge and sadhana, gave up every comfort and in 1964, in a small 6×7 foot room in the Manihar building, became absorbed in the sadhana of Maa Tripurasundari. Through complete surrender and childlike selfless love she received ‘Maa's’ boundless grace and divine darshan — ‘Maa’ accepted her as her beloved daughter.

After Bhaiyaji left the physical body (2003), she led all the spiritual and service work — the publishing of many books, and the widening of education, sanskar, women's empowerment, scholarships, free literature and food-grain assistance. On 7 May 2021 she left the physical body.

Born 1951, Sirsa (Punjab), into a family of modern outlook. Rich in independent thought and a reasoned view, Madhu Maa — though drawn to the spiritual presence of Bhaiyaji and Maa Basanti — wished to test her experience against reason, and so took an MA in Psychology.
