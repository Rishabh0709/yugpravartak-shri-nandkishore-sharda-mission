About the divine mission

From descent to unbroken service: a divine journey

For the welfare of humanity, under a special mission, Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari and Bhagwan Shiv (Babuji) sent their beloved son, Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda (Bhaiyaji), into this world.

In complete surrender, through true longing, firm resolve, severe sadhana, tireless effort and pure love, Bhaiyaji won the grace of Jagatjanani Maa and received her direct darshan, and taking ‘Maa’ herself as guru, received from her a true, eternal, universal, science-based Divya Gyan. He offered this supernatural knowledge to humanity through his timeless work ‘The Wonderful World After Death’. Suited to the modern scientific age, he brought together knowledge, devotion, selfless action and yoga to set out the ‘Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana’, so that a person, living life in joy, peace, happiness and love, may work for the welfare of their human life.

Under Bhaiyaji's guidance the spiritual path was opened for many sadhaks — including Sushri Basanti Manihar and Sushri Madhubala Advani (Madhu Maa) — and the ‘Manidweep Adhyatm Parivar’ was founded.

At ‘Maa's’ command, within Nishkam Karma Sadhana, Bhaiyaji began projects for public welfare such as education, sanskar, scholarships and women's empowerment. After he left the physical body, Maa Basanti Manihar, inspired by him, carried forward the publishing and free distribution of spiritual literature and many works of human service, all of which Madhu Maa now guides with care. Under her guidance the ‘Manidweep Adhyatm Parivar’ works on, in a spirit of steady devotion, for world brotherhood, world peace and human welfare.

Yugpravartak

Shri Nandkishore Sharda (Bhaiyaji)

Born in Jodhpur on 16 July 1944, Shri Nandkishore Sharda (Bhaiyaji) was a bright student of science and a person of radiant presence. At only 15, after his father's death, a deep detachment awoke within him before his father's burning pyre. In that moment he resolved that he would seek out this truth — what is the power that keeps the physical body moving? Who am I? Where have I come from? What is there after death? Does God exist?

To fulfil this resolve he put questions to many sadhus, saints and mahatmas, tantriks and mantriks, and read many religious books, but nowhere did he find a satisfying answer. At last, by the grace of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari, he found a wonderful peace in ‘Maa's’ temple behind the Mehrangarh fort. Pleased by his selfless love and single-minded worship, ‘Maa’ gave him her direct darshan and accepted him as her beloved son. On the ground of this divine experience, Bhaiyaji — a selfless karmayogi and a siddha of steady wisdom — gave, as today's age required in the material, social and spiritual spheres, a new way of thinking and new knowledge, and gave new life to the spirit of vasudhaiva kutumbakam, world brotherhood and world peace.

Tenderness and selfless surrender

The loving Maa Basanti Manihar

In 1964, under the guidance of Shri Nandkishore Sharda (Bhaiyaji), Maa Basanti Manihar took the spiritual path, and through complete surrender, selfless love and severe sadhana received the boundless grace, tenderness and divine darshan of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari. She made Bhaiyaji's deep and eternal knowledge simple, practical and accessible, carried it to countless seekers, and made the Manidweep Adhyatm Parivar a powerful means of spiritual awakening.

After Bhaiyaji left the physical body, she led all his spiritual and service work with success. Under her guidance spiritual books were published, and the varied projects of education, sanskar, women's empowerment, scholarships, food-grain assistance and human service were widened.

From sadhana to active leadership

The compassionate Maa Madhubala Advani

In 1970, under the loving guidance of the loving Maa Basanti Manihar and Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda (Bhaiyaji), Kumari Madhubala Advani (MA, B.Ed.) took the spiritual path and made the eternal knowledge her own. For more than five decades, under their care, through severe sadhana she completed the practices of many great conscious powers and received divine experiences and blessings.

She also received the sacred fortune of setting down Bhaiyaji's Divya Gyan as the timeless book ‘The Wonderful World After Death’. After Maa Basanti left the physical body in 2021, Madhu Maa took up the leadership of the Manidweep Adhyatm Parivar. She still guides all the spiritual and service projects actively and with care.

Under her guidance the family carries forward, along with the spread of the Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana, the tradition of education, sanskar, scholarships, women's empowerment, the free publishing of spiritual literature and human service.

A work of reference for Divya Gyan

The Wonderful World After Death

A divine unveiling of the eternal mysteries of life, death and consciousness.

‘The Wonderful World After Death’ is the timeless and original work of Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda (Bhaiyaji), in which he had set down, for the welfare of humanity, the eternal Divya Gyan received from Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari. It is not merely a book, but a singular work of reference for understanding the eternal truth of life, death, the soul and consciousness, on the ground of a coming-together of science and spirituality.

In this work, deep subjects such as the origin of the cosmos, the divine realms, brahma-particles, the physical and conscious body, the existence of God and the state after death are set out simply, with reason and in keeping with science. On the basis of his own experience and the Divya Gyan received from Jagatjanani Maa, Bhaiyaji offers answers to humanity's timeless questions.

A simple practice for today's age

The Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana

A simple practice that, awakening buddhi and vivek in modern life, leads towards material balance, spiritual progress and a joyful life.

A method for turning knowledge into life

Today information and knowledge are easily available, yet the lack of sound decisions, balanced vision and a life of discernment keeps growing. According to Bhaiyaji, God has given a human being two priceless gifts — buddhi and vivek; when buddhi becomes pure, vivek awakens and gives a positive direction to thought, decision, character and the way of seeing life.

The Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana is a whole path that awakens and refines these two powers — one that, even while living in modern life, gives a direction of material balance, spiritual progress and human welfare.

The divine moment of taking up the chakra

The start of a change of age

“Now I take up the chakra.”

In 1990, during a spiritual journey through South India, when Bhaiyaji and some members of the ‘Maa Parivar’ reached Goa, on a rock amid the sea Bhaiyaji spoke these words, calm and grave. With them, calling on the divine powers, he took the symbolic posture of taking up the chakra. In that moment his luminous form and the awareness rising from him became an unforgettable divine experience for those present. The whole atmosphere throbbed with joy, peace and divine energy.

The taking up of the chakra was the highest fruition of his long sadhana, the knowledge gained on his spiritual journeys, the divine conscious powers he had received, and the grace of Jagatjanani Maa. It was not merely a spiritual event, but the divine moment of taking up the chakra for human welfare, the start of a new age, an awakening of thought grounded in buddhi and vivek, and the founding of world brotherhood and world peace.

After the taking up of the chakra, along with the spiritual sadhana of knowledge and devotion, karma-sadhana — selfless service for public welfare — was also joined in. In this same course, in 1992 Jagatjanani Maa said to Maa Basanti, “A house has been made for you.”

A sadhana-sthali built at Maa's command

The building of Manidweep

Bhaiyaji and Maa Basanti were absorbed in sadhana and spiritual reflection at the Manihar building. When Jagatjanani Maa gave the command to build a new house, they were astonished. After the taking up of the chakra, karma-sadhana called for the collective participation of many people, and the space at the Manihar building was not enough for this.

In 1995 the building at A-183, Shastri Nagar was ready, and at ‘Maa's’ command it was named ‘Manidweep’ — the name of Maa's divine realm. On 10 June 1995 the members of the Maa Parivar moved from the Manihar building to Manidweep.

Manidweep is not merely a building.

It is a centre full of faith, sadhana, love, awareness and sanskar — a sadhana-sthali built at the command of a Maa, where simply sitting with a calm mind awakens positive thought, joy, compassion, self-restraint and the spirit of ‘vasudhaiva kutumbakam’.
