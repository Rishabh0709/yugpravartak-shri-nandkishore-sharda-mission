How it was set down

Knowledge that flowed beneath a tree near the Bhootnath temple

The Divya Gyan that Bhaiya Shri Nandkishore Sharda received by taking Jagatjanani Maa as his guru began to be set down, once Maa gave her command, from May 2002 as the divine book The Wonderful World After Death. How Bhaiyaji came to receive this knowledge — the whole journey of sadhana — read here →

Bhaiyaji had this book set down while seated beneath a tree near the Bhootnath temple. He and Madhu Maa would go there; Bhaiyaji would fall into deep meditation and his contact with the world of consciousness would open. He would then speak in a continuous flow, which Madhu Maa wrote down. At that time his face would take on a divine radiance.

This book is a divine work that gives right guidance to a generation dazzled and confused by today's scientific and material glare.
The book's vision

A deep account of spirituality, on the ground of science

In this book, Adhyatmyogi Shri Nandkishore Sharda presents the Divya Gyan he received by taking Maa Tripurasundari as his guru, together with his own experiences, from a scientific point of view. It explains as a whole such subjects as the structure of the cosmos, the origin of creation, nature, the making of the divine realms, the existence of God, the purpose of human life and the power of consciousness.

The idea of brahma-particles, of the physical and the conscious body, the realms of the gods, the existence of divine messengers and many mysteries of the world of consciousness are also brought to light. This knowledge awakens in a person a firm faith and trust in God and in the power of consciousness.

It helps one to see that material comforts are necessary for life, but that the real goal of human life is spiritual progress and the attainment of God.

This divine work of Bhaiyaji is a nectar-like, supernatural gift to humanity. For anyone curious about the mysteries of the cosmos, it is an original work of reference.
What the book covers

A chapter-by-chapter guide

What comes after death? On this question, as old as time, Bhaiyaji sought to look beyond the horizon — within the stream of the Indian rishi tradition, of science and of spiritual reflection.

Chapter one

The question of death and human birth

Drawing on the reflection of ancestors, saints and science about death, the question arises: how does a human infant, dependent at birth, come to be a Vivekananda, an Einstein — or a person who turns the other way?

Chapter two

The foundation of science

Explaining the basic principles of science in simple language, it discusses the possible locations of the divine realms and the scientific role of spirituality.

Chapter three

Physical and conscious particles

From the properties of the basic particles of the physical world and the world of consciousness, and their natural difference, it seeks to establish the supreme God through science.

Chapter four

Light, nature and darshan

Explaining the play of nature through the special properties of light, it reflects on the mystery of an object and its reflected image, and on the possibility of Swami Vivekananda's darshan of Maa Kali.

Chapter five

The state after death

When a person separates from the earth's gravitational pull, what is their state, what do they leave behind on earth, and what is their form? This is what the chapter considers.

Chapter six

The making of the divine realms

Along with the mystery of what lay before the Planck time — the final limit of science — it seeks to explain the process by which the divine realms and the divine conscious bodies are formed.

Chapter seven

Answering the questions

On the basis of the earlier chapters, the likely questions in a reader's mind are addressed, so that study may turn into reflection and contemplation.

The Gyan Ganga

From resolve to reality

By the end of 2002, the writing of the book based on the Divya Gyan Bhaiya Shri Nandkishore Sharda received from Maa was complete. His heartfelt wish was that this knowledge should not stay confined to one book, but reach everyone and turn humanity towards spiritual truth.

With that purpose he had conceived the Gyan Ganga Mission. After Bhaiyaji left the physical body in January 2003, Maa Basanti carried the resolve forward and founded the Shri Nandkishore Sharda Gyan Ganga Mission in April 2003. Through this mission the divine book was published in November 2003.

May 2002

The work of setting down the Divya Gyan begins.

April 2003

Maa Basanti founds the Gyan Ganga Mission.

November 2003

The book is published and the knowledge begins to reach everyone.

What Divya Gyan has done

From doubt towards faith and self-knowledge

The Divya Gyan held in this book has given many lives a new direction, vision and meaning. Through studying it, thousands have sought to understand the purpose of human life, the existence of God and the nature of the power of consciousness.

This knowledge has resolved the doubts and spiritual questions of many seekers. A number of people who doubted the existence of God were, through its effect, turned towards faith, and began to shape their lives to spiritual values.

This book does not merely give information; it calls the reader to question, to reflect, and to walk towards self-knowledge.
