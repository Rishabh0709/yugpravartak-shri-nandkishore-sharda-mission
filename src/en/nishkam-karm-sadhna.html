Nishkam Karma Sadhana

A selfless stream of service for human welfare

A sacred stream of education, sanskar, self-reliance, human service and Divya Gyan, flowing from one far-sighted resolve. This page shows that the service projects are not separate efforts, but the coordinated journey of a single selfless practice.

Education→Sanskar→Self-reliance→Service→Whole development
A far-sighted resolve

Behind the service, a vision of whole human development

All the service work of Shri Nandkishore Sharda, the loving Maa Basanti Manihar and Kumari Madhubala Advani is inspired by Jagatjanani Maa's command and by the wide vision of life in her karma-sadhana. For them, karma is not duty or social service alone, but a practice of self-development, self-purification and the good of the world. At its root, therefore, lay not some chance plan but a considered, long-term and far-sighted vision of the whole development of a human being.

They believed that the real development of society is not possible through financial aid or formal education alone. A person's life needs sanskar along with education, discernment along with knowledge, sensitivity along with prosperity, self-respect along with self-reliance, and social responsibility along with personal progress.

The highest form of the spiritual reveals itself in service.

In Bhaiyaji's view, serving humanity with a selfless spirit is a living extension of sadhana itself. In that spirit, education, sanskar, spiritual awakening, women's empowerment, self-reliance, human service and the spread of knowledge were joined to one another.

From resolve to a journey of service

The vision came first; the work took shape after

Different trusts were formed as time and circumstance required, but at their root lay one spirit: to make human life educated, well-formed, self-reliant, healthy, sensitive and spiritually responsible.

When the spiritual enters life, it becomes service.

This page therefore sets out the service work trust by trust, so that the reader can see which need each piece of work began from, and how it came to be joined to the wider welfare of humanity.

The coordinated journey of the bodies

Four bodies, one whole vision

Each body was given a distinct role, and every role stayed joined to the same founding philosophy.

1

Swami Vivekanand Students' Welfare Charitable Trust

The ground of school-level education and sanskar for girls

Founded on 10 June 1996, this trust laid the first stone of the far-sighted plan: educate a girl, give her sanskar, and awaken her confidence.

School scholarships

A means for girls kept out of education by financial hardship to stay in school.

Sanskar and confidence

Joining education to character, moral values and a positive way of seeing life.
2

Maa Shardamani Trust

From college education to higher study and the making of a future

Founded in 2001, this trust is the next link after the first. Where school education laid a firm foundation, this carried girls on to higher education and further opportunity.

Higher education support

Financial and academic support for college-level girl students in need.

Opportunities for the future

Joining education to self-respect, self-reliance and a bright future.
3

Gyanyogi Shri Nandkishore Sharda Adhyatm Kendra

A wide dimension of boys' education, whole-person growth and human service

The Kendra's role grew beyond education, to health, family support, women's self-reliance and whole-person development.

Support for boys' education

Help and guidance for students in need towards higher and professional education.

Food assistance

An effort that began with 50 families during Covid-19 and later reached nearly 300.

Yoga, pranayama and meditation

Regular practice towards physical, mental, emotional and spiritual balance.

Women's self-reliance

Free training, self-employment and a path to a dignified livelihood through the sewing centre founded in 2025.
4

Shri Nandkishore Sharda Gyan Ganga Mission

The spread of Divya Gyan and spiritual literature

This mission's charge is to publish and freely spread Bhaiyaji's spiritual knowledge, vision of life and inspiring literature, so that consciousness may widen through thought and knowledge as well.

Read about the Gyan Ganga Mission in depth

Publishing spiritual literature

Carrying spiritual knowledge, reflection and papers to present and future generations.

Free distribution

Making Divya Gyan easily available to everyone is itself a form of selfless service.
A whole vision of service

The four main dimensions of selfless service

The work of the four bodies looks different, but its essence rests on four founding dimensions: education, sanskar, inner balance, and self-reliant human service.

1

Education and scholarships

Support for school, higher and professional education, so that financial want does not block the path.

2

Sanskar and personality development

Joining discernment, a sense of duty, character-building and a positive outlook to education.

3

Yoga, health and inner balance

Making life steady and content through yoga, pranayama, meditation and mental-emotional balance.

4

Human service and self-reliance

Food-grain assistance, family support, skill-building and help towards a dignified life.

Proof of foresight

From service, the whole development of society

Beginning with school education for girls and reaching on to higher education, boys' education, professional education, sanskar, yoga and meditation, family support, women's skill-building and spiritual literature — this journey is drawn by one shared purpose and direction.

From education, sanskar;
from sanskar, character;
from character, self-reliance;
from self-reliance, service;
and from service, the whole development of society.
