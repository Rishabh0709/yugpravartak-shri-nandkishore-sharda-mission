The first step

How to begin sadhana

Buddhi-Vivek Yog Sadhna is a simple practice followed while living a full householder's life. No prior qualification, initiation or fee is needed to begin — only a genuine inquiry. Below are the practical steps to begin and common questions from seekers joining for the first time. For deeper questions on spirituality and Divya Gyan, see Spiritual Q&A.

Practical steps

Beginning right where you are

  1. Read

    Begin with Bhaiyaji's timeless work 'The Wonderful World After Death' — a scientific, logical treatment of the cosmos, the power of consciousness, the existence of God, and the state after death. The book is available free at Manidweep.

  2. Reflect

    Before accepting what you read, ask questions, weigh it against reason, and think through every aspect with seriousness. This is the order of 'knowledge → understanding → reflection', through which the intellect grows clear and discernment awakens.

  3. Seek guidance

    Come to Manidweep or the Siddhapeeth for personal guidance. Here, each seeker is guided towards a sadhana method suited to their own inquiry, intellectual capacity and personal circumstances.

  4. Practise

    Carry the discernment awakened by knowledge into life as resolve, devotion and mindful action. This is not a mandatory checklist — the six steps flow naturally, one into the next. The more sincerity and practice, the more genuine the happiness and peace.

Where to learn

Places of sadhana

Manidweep

A-183, Shastri Nagar, Jodhpur, Rajasthan

A place of sadhana full of faith, practice and living consciousness. Simply sitting here in a peaceful mind is itself the beginning of sadhana.

Open daily from 10 AM to 8 PM — everyone is welcome.

Spiritual discussions — every Sunday evening, 7:00 to 8:30 PM.

Adhyatmyogi Shri Nandkishore Sharda Sadhna-Sthali (Siddhapeeth)

Kabootaron Ka Chowk, Jodhpur

The sacred site where Sharda Ji practised sadhana and received knowledge from Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari.

Free yoga, pranayama and meditation — daily from 7:15 to 8:15 AM, under the guidance of a trained yoga teacher.

What to expect

A few things made clear

  • No fee — guidance, the sadhana spaces, yoga classes and all the sacred literature are entirely free. No membership or initiation fee.
  • Your householder life continues — no need to leave home, family or work. Not a renunciation of worldly life, but its balance and purification.
  • Open to everyone — beyond the boundaries of caste, religion, sect, age or background.
  • At your own pace — guidance is shaped to your own inquiry and circumstances; there is no fixed timeline.
