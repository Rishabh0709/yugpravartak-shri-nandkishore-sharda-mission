A service born of foresight

From school to higher education

After the Swami Vivekanand Students' Welfare Charitable Trust was founded, Bhaiya Shri Nandkishore Sharda and Maa Basanti saw that school-level education alone was not enough for a girl's all-round growth and a bright future.

Girls needed the chance of higher education, and with it the sanskar and values that make a person confident, discerning, self-reliant and a responsible citizen. From this foresight, the Maa Shardamani Trust was founded on 28 March 2001.

The true purpose of education

Beyond a scholarship — towards the making of a life

The trust's purpose was to give girls selected at school level a scholarship for higher study, along with free university-level textbooks, so that financial want would not block their path in education. Its working method, its approach to selection and its rules were kept the same as the Swami Vivekanand Students' Welfare Charitable Trust — scholarships tied to financial need, not to marks, with no distinction of caste, religion or community.

This effort was never limited to helping a girl earn a degree. Bhaiyaji and Maa Basanti held that education is complete only when it gives sanskar and discernment along with knowledge. In that spirit, Sanskar classes were arranged for the girls.

True empowerment of women

A woman of knowledge, of sanskar, and of responsibility

The greatest achievement of the Maa Shardamani Trust is just this: it has joined education to scholarship, scholarship to self-reliance, and self-reliance to sanskar and responsibility.

The aim here is not merely to educate a girl, but to shape a woman who is learned and also well-formed, confident and also humble, self-reliant and also responsible towards her family and society.

This is the real purpose of education, and this is the true empowerment of women.

The Maa Shardamani Trust carries forward the spirit of Bhaiyaji and Maa Basanti, in which education, sanskar and self-reliance together become the ground of society's whole development.
