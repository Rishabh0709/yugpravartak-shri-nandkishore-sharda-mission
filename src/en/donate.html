Why there is no payment gateway

A gateway would deduct a fee from every donation. Direct bank transfer or UPI means your entire contribution reaches the work you chose. It asks for one extra moment of care from you — see the steps below.

Giving safely

A few things to keep in mind

Verify before you send

The beneficiary name your bank or UPI app shows should match the trust named on this page. Pause and contact us if it does not.

Protect your credentials

The Mission will never ask for your banking password, card PIN, UPI PIN or one-time password.

Contributions from outside India

Please contact the Mission before sending any contribution from abroad, so the correct route and current compliance position can be confirmed.
