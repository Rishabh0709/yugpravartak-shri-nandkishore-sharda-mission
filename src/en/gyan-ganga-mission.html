Literature, Divya Gyan and service

Shri Nandkishore Sharda Gyan Ganga Mission

A sacred stream that publishes and carries to everyone the priceless knowledge, reflections and papers given by Bhaiya Shri Nandkishore Sharda. Alongside the spread of knowledge, the mission also supports the development of educational institutions.

Why it was founded

Keeping the stream of knowledge flowing without a break

After Bhaiyaji left the physical body, Maa Basanti and Madhu Maa founded this mission for the publishing and free distribution of spiritual literature. Its main purpose is to publish and spread Bhaiyaji's priceless knowledge, reflections, papers such as his personal diary, and spiritual guidance, so that present and future generations may be rightly guided — and to consider how this knowledge might spread in India and abroad, so that a new age can begin.

The mission has published works connected to Bhaiyaji's Divya Gyan, his life, his handwritten diary, the personality of Maa Basanti, and the spiritual stream of Manidweep. The aim of these books is to awaken in the reader faith, discernment, self-reflection and a spiritual sense of life's direction.

Published literature

2003 to 2026 — the tradition of books from Bhaiyaji's knowledge

The main works published by the mission. All books are available free of charge to seeking readers at Manidweep.

  1. The Spiritual Man of Manidweep: Shri Nandkishore Sharda

    2003 · 2006 · 2011

    A memorial volume drawn from the recollections of more than 300 people. Editor: Prof. (Dr.) Ramprasad Dadhich ‘Prasad’; co-editor: Sushri Madhubala Advani.

  2. Mrityu ke Baad ka Alaukik Sansar (The Wonderful World After Death)

    2003 · English edition 2004

Bringing together the basic principles of science and the insight of spirituality, it examines the cosmos, the power of consciousness, the divine realms and the state after death.   3. Chakradhari Yugpravartak Adhyatmyogi Shri Nandkishore Sharda, beloved of Maa Tripurasundari

    2015

    Editor: Prof. (Dr.) Ramprasad Dadhich ‘Prasad’; co-editors: Sushri Basanti Manihar and the ‘Manidweep’ Adhyatm Parivar. An authentic and wide-ranging record of Bhaiyaji's personality, work and original spiritual thought.

  4. A Lamp-Flame of Manidweep — Jiji Basanti Manihar

    2016

    Author: Prof. (Dr.) Ramprasad Dadhich ‘Prasad’. Based on first-hand experience, a blend of prose and verse — an introduction to Maa Basanti's faith, sadhana and selfless service.

  5. Shri Nandkishore Sharda — the light-bearer of the Siddhapeeth, herald of joy, peace, prosperity and welfare within worldly life

    2020

    Author: Prof. (Dr.) Ramprasad Dadhich ‘Prasad’. Based on four decades of close companionship and composed in free verse (2019) — a portrait of Bhaiyaji's personality, sadhana, thought and karmayoga.

  6. Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda (Bhaiya), beloved of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari

    2022 · 2025

    Compiled by the ‘Manidweep’ Adhyatm Parivar. A short, authentic introduction to Bhaiyaji's jnana yoga, bhakti yoga, nishkam karmayoga and his mission for human welfare.

  7. A Lamp-Flame of ‘Manidweep’, beloved of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari — the loving Maa Basanti Manihar

    Editor: Prof. (Dr.) V. K. Bhansali; co-editors: Sushri Madhubala Advani and the ‘Manidweep’ Adhyatm Parivar. A work that presents Bhaiyaji's spiritual knowledge and Maa Basanti's life as one inseparable stream.

  8. The loving Maa Basanti Manihar, beloved of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari — a life of surrender

    2025

    Compiled by the ‘Manidweep’ Adhyatm Parivar. Like ‘an ocean in a cup’ — the practical way in which Maa Basanti brought knowledge into daily life, useful for every age group.

  9. Daivya Dainandini — the handwritten diary of Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda

    2026

    Compiled by the ‘Manidweep’ Adhyatm Parivar. The feelings, questions, struggles and spiritual experiences Bhaiyaji wrote — for himself alone — from the age of twelve. After he left the physical body, Maa Basanti published it at Jagatjanani Maa's command.

Through this trust there is also provision to publish socially useful religious books by great figures, written by others. The mission's aim is that the stream of the Gyan Ganga keeps flowing, without a break, into countless people — and so these books are available to readers free of charge.

From knowledge to the learning environment

Trying to clear the quiet obstacles in education

The Gyan Ganga Mission has not been limited to the spread of knowledge. Bhaiyaji held that giving a scholarship alone does not complete the service of education. If a school lacks enough classrooms, seating, water, sanitation or a safe environment, students — girls especially — face many quiet obstacles in learning. Inspired by the mission, construction and support work was therefore carried out in educational institutions, so that students could learn in an environment of dignity, comfort and confidence.

Selected contributions

Major construction and support work

The spirit behind this work was that education is not complete through fees or books alone; it also needs an environment, facilities and institutional support.

Reception room and classrooms

The ‘Shri Nandkishore Sharda Reception Room’ and three classrooms built at Mahalakshmi Girls Senior Secondary School, Pratap Nagar.

Commerce education

Financial support for building the ‘Kumari Basanti Manihar Commerce Block’ at the Women's College, Jodhpur.

Tulsi drinking-water stand

Drinking-water facilities built in girls' schools in memory of Smt. Tulsi Devi.

Classroom furniture

Furniture provided for 16 classrooms across 7 girls' schools, to make everyday study easier.

Computer lab

Support for facilities such as computer labs, to connect students to modern learning resources.

Safety and basic facilities

Boundary walls, sanitation, entrance arrangements and other necessary work helped make schools safe, healthy and complete places of learning.
