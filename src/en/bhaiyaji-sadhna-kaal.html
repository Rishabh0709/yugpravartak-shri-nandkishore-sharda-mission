Bhaiyaji's period of sadhana

Bhaiyaji's journey of sadhana

In an age lost to the darkness of tamoguna, materialism and ignorance, Jagatjanani Maa sent her beloved Shri Nandkishore Sharda to this earth to kindle the light of knowledge and give humanity a simple, universal spiritual path suited to this age.

The age's call

A resolve to carry humanity from darkness to light

In this age, Tamoguna is becoming increasingly dominant, while Rajoguna and Satoguna are gradually declining. Humanity is losing its way amid the dazzling allure of materialism, the spread of moral degradation, and the darkness of ignorance.

At such a time, to dispel this darkness through a simple, effective and universal form of knowledge suited to the needs of the present age, and to fulfil this divine mission, Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari sent Her two beloved children — Shri Nandkishore Sharda and Kumari Basanti Manihar — into the world.

Through this mission, humanity is progressing towards the establishment of Satyuga in Kaliyuga.
Birth

A radiant life begins on the sacred ground of Jodhpur

Shri Nandkishore Sharda was born on 16 July 1944 in the sacred land of Jodhpur, into the prosperous and devout family of Smt. Tulsi Devi and Shri Vijaykishan Ji Sharda. From an early age, he possessed an intense thirst for knowledge, a sharp intellect, and a deep interest in literature.

His personality was striking, while his thinking was philosophical, scientific and logical. His extraordinary oratory, profound emotional expression and an unusual spiritual magnetism were distinctive features of his multifaceted personality, captivating all who listened to him. Because of his simple and pure thoughts and his deeply affectionate nature, everyone lovingly addressed him as Bhaiya or Bhaiya Ji.

The extraordinary diary

A diary begun in childhood reveals his essential nature

From childhood, Bhaiyaji was endowed with the noble qualities of renunciation and love. He was an exceptionally sensitive and compassionate person, imbued with simplicity, firm resolve, selfless service, unwavering dedication to his goal, and deep empathy for the suffering of others. A glimpse of these qualities can be found in his handwritten diary:

“Today's society judges a person solely on the basis of his material success. If a person is successful in worldly terms, he is considered intelligent; otherwise, he is regarded as a fool. In this way, society has placed me in the category of a ‘fool.’ In reality, I do not wish to become worldly-wise, for there is no inner peace in such a life.”
Nandkishore Sharda

Bhaiyaji's diary is an important record of his journey of divine consciousness. We are fortunate that his original, authentic diary, begun at the tender age of twelve, is preserved to this day, its authenticity beyond doubt. It is a pure reflection of his thoughts, contemplation, spiritual practice, surrender, personal experiences and quest for truth — the events recorded in it belong to the very time they occurred, not accounts written in hindsight.

1959 · The search for the conscious power

A search for truth that began with "Who am I?"

In 1959, when he was only fifteen years old, his father passed away. This event brought about a profound transformation in his life. That very night, he recorded deeply moving reflections in his diary.

“….Today, I see those flames rising from the funeral pyre before my eyes. What a strange illusion this life is. No one truly belongs to anyone, and no one is truly mine. Even this body is not mine. Who am I? Where have I come from? Where will I go?… I have to find out. Who am I, and who is guiding me? Where has my father gone? …Who are my real parents?…”

The fact that he wrote this diary entry on the very day of his father's passing is extraordinary — to give such immediate, authentic expression to his thoughts in a moment of such overwhelming grief reflects an unusual inner composure. He had never before set foot in a cremation ground; watching his father's mortal remains consigned to the flames shook him to the very core of his being. There, at the cremation ground itself, he made a firm resolve to discover the reality of the conscious power that animates the physical body.

His deep interest in spiritual literature led him to study the teachings of almost all major religions, and in his search for Truth he met saints, mahatmas, tantrics and even those who claimed supernatural powers — yet nowhere did he find an answer that truly satisfied him. In the course of this search he came into contact with a tantric who would have currency notes delivered inside a sealed matchbox; the experience strengthened his belief that unseen forces truly existed, and he reasoned that behind such countless powers there had to be one Supreme Power — God, or Jagatjanani Maa. Eventually he concluded that he alone would have to find the answer to this mystery. With no guru, no scripture and no guide, he set out alone on an unknown path, and despite many obstacles kept moving steadily towards his goal in the spirit of ‘Ekla Chalo Re’ — walk alone.

In truth, it was Jagatjanani Maa Herself who was preparing Bhaiyaji to emerge as a Yugpravartak, and for this purpose She kept him under Her unseen protection, allowing him to pass through circumstances that tested him in every possible way.
Tekri Maa · the beginning of sadhana

Boundless peace found in an awakened temple, and a pull towards Maa

One day, while wandering around Jodhpur Fort in deep contemplation, he felt inexplicably drawn towards Tekri, a hill nearby, and reached the place almost instinctively. The moment he entered the cave-like temple there, he experienced an immense sense of peace, and on having the darshan of the image of Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari, he became deeply absorbed in meditation. From that day on, he made the place his sadhana-sthali and began spending most of his time there.

The Tekri temple held the immeasurable power, radiance and consciousness of Jagatjanani Maa. In Bhaiyaji was a resolve to find truth that had grown unwavering, a complete surrender, renunciation and passion. There, at Maa Tripurasundari's feet, he surrendered himself entirely and, accepting Her as his guide, began his intense sadhana day and night with unwavering faith and devotion.

“Maa Bhavani, please accept my salutations. From today, You are my guide. I leave everything in Your hands and place my entire trust in You.”
Your son, Kishore

He spent night after night at Tekri, immersed in the worship and sadhana of the divine Mother — it was here that his intense spiritual journey truly began. For days at a time he remained without adequate food or water, sustaining himself on little more than a quarter-kilo of spinach a day, shedding tears at Maa's awakened temple as he longed for Her darshan.

As this bond deepened, his very signature evolved to mirror it — from Nandkishore Sharda, to Kishore, and finally to Kishore, Maa's beloved — each change marking the ever-deepening intimacy of his sadhana and his union with Maa.

Complete surrender

When Maa gave darshan, Bhaiyaji held fast to her feet

With a spirit of complete selflessness, Bhaiyaji established a mother-child bond with Jagatjanani Maa and surrendered himself entirely to Her. In Her absence he would yearn intensely and cry out, “Maa, give me Your darshan.”

After prolonged sadhana, when he did not receive Her darshan, he realised that even spiritual desires can become an obstacle on the path — so he surrendered not only his wish for darshan but the very purpose and resolve of his life to Maa, feeling only that he loved Her, and would go on loving Her. In an act of complete surrender, like a child surrendering himself entirely to his mother, he symbolically performed his own last rites before Maa and accepted Jagatjanani Maa as his true Mother.

“…The Mother whom I sometimes call Parvardigar, sometimes Mummy, and sometimes Maa is my Mother, my Guru, and my everything. I have placed my body, mind, wealth, life, and all my love at Her feet. In truth, worshipping Her feet and loving Her is the very purpose of my life. When She blessed me with Her darshan, I ran to Her and clasped Her feet. She alone is my everything…”

Bhaiyaji's diary reveals the depth of his bond with Maa. He regarded Her as his Guru, Mother and everything, surrendering his entire being at Her feet — he had no existence separate from Her, dissolving into Maa as milk dissolves into sugar.

When Bhaiyaji received the darshan of Jagatjanani Maa, he was about 18 years of age. This was in itself a wondrous spiritual event.
Divine knowledge given by Maa

Self-realisation of consciousness, the cosmos and the divine realms

After the darshan, Maa accepted him as her son and, becoming his guru herself, had him undertake the highest levels of divine sadhana. Maa gave him self-realisation of consciousness and the structure of the cosmos, the structure of divine particles, the concept of the two bodies, the origin of the conscious body, and the creation of the divine realms.

In time, Bhaiyaji had this very nectar-like knowledge set down in the divine, original text "The Wonderful World After Death." This knowledge is not mere thought, but the light of realisation attained through his austerity, surrender, sadhana and Maa's grace.

Bhaiyaji's journey of sadhana still guides seekers today
