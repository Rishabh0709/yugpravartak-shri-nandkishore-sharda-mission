Weekly spiritual guidance

Spiritual Knowledge Discourses

In the relentless pursuit of material success, there are moments when a person pauses and looks back, only to realise that something is still missing from life — the mind feels restless and devoid of true joy. A question then arises: where can one find lasting peace, genuine happiness and inner fulfilment? One answer lies in the Spiritual Knowledge Discourses held at Manidweep every Sunday evening. Begun under Maa Basanti Ji's care, this tradition continues today under Madhu Maa's guidance.

The foundation

Bhaiyaji's divine wisdom — suited to life today

The knowledge that Yugpravartak Bhaiya Shri Nandkishore Ji Sharda received from Jagatjanani Maa has been presented in a form that responds to the social environment, intellectual development and contemporary needs of human beings. This knowledge is eternal, simple, natural, practical, systematic, logical, sattvic, meaningful and universal.

It offers an integrated understanding of material and spiritual life rather than treating them as separate. Thus, an individual can experience happiness and fulfilment in worldly life while also progressing towards his or her spiritual aspirations.

Format

What happens in these discussions

These Spiritual Knowledge Discourses are held every Sunday from 7:00 to 8:30 p.m. at Manidweep. They explore how Bhaiyaji's divine knowledge can be understood and applied in daily life through a rational and practical approach. Through the Buddhi-Vivek Yog Sadhana methodology, guidance is offered on various dimensions of human life from a spiritual perspective. Discussions focus on understanding everyday challenges, managing mental stress, nurturing healthy family relationships and finding balanced approaches to personal difficulties.

  • Particular emphasis is placed on awakening intellect and discrimination (Buddhi-Vivek), positive thinking, ethical living, self-reflection and self-discipline.
  • Participants are encouraged to nurture mutual respect, love and open communication within the family, and to resolve differences with understanding.
  • Maintaining a balanced perspective in challenging situations.
  • Cultivating noble qualities within, and progressing from humanity towards divinity.

The discussions inspire participants to live with joy, peace and inner fulfilment, seek a deeper connection with the Divine and move towards the true purpose of human life.

Effect

From the self to society

Those who regularly reflect upon and imbibe the insights shared in these Spiritual Knowledge Discourses are not merely creating a more balanced and stress-free life for themselves; they are also becoming active contributors to the well-being of their families, society, nation and the wider world.

Thus, these Spiritual Knowledge Discourses bring together Buddhi-Vivek, self-development and service, becoming a means of nurturing broader human values such as universal brotherhood, world peace and Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam — the belief that the entire world is one family.

Want to join?

These discussions are open to everyone, entirely free. Come to Manidweep on any Sunday evening.
