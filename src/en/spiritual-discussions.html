Weekly spiritual guidance

Spiritual discussions

Even amid worldly achievement, when the mind feels restless and joyless, a question arises — where does true, lasting peace lie? In search of that answer, spiritual discussions are held at Manidweep every Sunday evening. Begun under Maa Basanti Ji's care, this tradition continues today under Madhu Maa's guidance.

The foundation

Bhaiyaji's Divya Gyan — suited to life today

The knowledge Yugpravartak Bhaiya Shri Nandkishore Ji Sharda received from Jagatjanani Maa in this age is grounded in today's social context, in humanity's intellectual development and needs. This knowledge is true, eternal, simple, natural, practical, systematic, reasoned, wholesome, meaningful and universal.

This self-realised knowledge does not see worldly and spiritual life as separate, but as one whole. This is why a person can remain joyful in worldly life while also attaining their spiritual goal.

Format

What happens in these discussions

Held every Sunday from 7:00 to 8:30 PM, these discussions teach how to bring Bhaiyaji's Divya Gyan into practice on a scientific footing. Through the Buddhi-Vivek Yog Sadhna method, guidance is given on various aspects of human life from a spiritual perspective, and discussion takes place on understanding and finding balanced solutions to daily challenges, mental stress, family relationships and personal problems.

  • Special emphasis on awakening discernment, positive thinking, ethics, self-reflection and self-control.
  • Encouragement to maintain mutual respect, love and communication within the family, and to resolve differences with understanding.
  • Adopting a composed outlook in stressful situations.
  • Developing virtue within oneself, moving from humanity towards divinity.

The ultimate aim is to live with joy, peace and happiness while striving to attain God — fulfilling the fundamental purpose of human life.

Effect

From the self to society

Seekers who reflect on and hold to these discussions regularly are not only building stress-free lives for themselves, but are also playing an active role in the welfare of family, society, nation and world.

In this way these spiritual discussions join discernment, self-development and service together, becoming a means of contributing towards the broader human values of world-brotherhood, world-peace, and 'Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam' — the world as one family.

Want to join?

These discussions are open to everyone, entirely free. Come to Manidweep on any Sunday evening.
