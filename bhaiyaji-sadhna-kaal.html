Bhaiyaji’s Journey of Sadhna

In an age of material confusion, Jagadjanani Maa awakened through Bhaiyaji a simple and universal path.

Inner Call

A Resolve that Led from Darkness to Light

His Sadhna began as an uncompromising search for truth.

Early Life

A Radiant Life in Tapobhumi Jodhpur

Born in Jodhpur on 16 July 1944, he possessed sharp intellect and deep spiritual curiosity.

Diary

His Diary Reveals His Inner Nature

His handwritten diary, begun at twelve, records his struggle, inquiry and spiritual progress.

“I must search: who am I, and who is moving me?”
Truth

The Search Began with “Who Am I?”

At his father’s cremation he resolved to know the conscious power behind life.

Tekri

Peace at the Awakened Temple

At Tekri, before Maa Tripura Sundari, he found deep peace and accepted the place as his Sadhna-sthal.

Darshan

When Maa Granted Darshan

After deep surrender, Maa gave Darshan and Bhaiyaji held Her feet.

“Maa is my Mother, my Guru and my everything.”
Experience

Consciousness, Universe and Divine Realms

Maa guided him into direct experience of consciousness and divine realms.

Living Guidance

Bhaiyaji’s Sadhna Journey Still Gives Direction

The journey from inquiry to surrender continues to assure seekers that sincere love, disciplined reflection and Maa’s grace can transform life even in the present age.

His Sadhna Still Guides Seekers

His journey assures seekers that Divine grace is possible in this age.
