How to read the student figures

As of 31 March 2026, student counts represent cumulative instances of scholarship support across reporting periods. A student supported in more than one period may therefore appear more than once; these figures should not be read as unique individuals.

Continuity matters

The programme began in 2020 with monthly assistance for 50 families and gradually expanded to 300 families. As families regain stability, they voluntarily make room for others facing greater need.

Figures represent assistance distributed cumulatively since the programme began.
