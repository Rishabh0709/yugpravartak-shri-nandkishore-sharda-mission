Reading with Care

What these figures show—and what they do not

Responsible impact communication includes the basis of each number. Detailed reports will identify periods, units and whether a figure represents individual people, beneficiary records or financial assistance.

01

Cumulative, not annual

Headline figures represent support accumulated over time. They should not be interpreted as the activity of a single year.

02

Programme-specific

Each Trust maintains its own purpose and records. Figures remain attached to the work that produced them.

03

People before totals

Numbers establish scale; stories, methods and long-term relationships explain the transformation behind them.
