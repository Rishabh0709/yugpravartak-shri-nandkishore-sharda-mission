Questions by Theme

Read these questions as a beginning for deeper reflection.

What is the purpose of human life?

To know truth, cultivate virtues and move from humanity toward divinity.

What is Buddhi-Vivek Yog Sadhna?

A practical method to refine intellect and awaken discernment.

Can this path be followed in family life?

Yes, it is meant for people living in the world.

What is Nishkam Karma?

Action performed as an offering without selfish attachment.

Why is Manidweep significant?

It is the living centre of guidance, Sadhna, Sanskar and service.

Who was Bhaiyaji?

A seeker, philosopher and guide who received divine knowledge from Maa Tripura Sundari.

Who was Maa Basanti Ji?

A motherly guide who carried Bhaiyaji’s knowledge and service forward.

Who is Madhu Maa?

The present guide carrying forward the Mission’s activities.
