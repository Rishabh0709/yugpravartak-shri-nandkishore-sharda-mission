The Circle of Seva

Supported once.
Serving forward.

Opportunity is not meant to end with one life. When students become self-reliant, many freely choose to mentor, teach, volunteer or support another learner—allowing gratitude to become purposeful service.
A Self-Sustaining Cycle of Service

One supported journey can open the way for another

The circle begins with need-based support, but it is strengthened through Sanskar, mentoring and spiritual grounding. Giving forward is never treated as repayment; it is a voluntary expression of gratitude, confidence and Seva.

Service Forward

An alumna may teach, mentor, volunteer or sponsor another student, beginning the circle anew.

Education opens the door. Sanskar nurtures the desire to hold that door open for another.

ND
A Living Example

Neetu Deora: from receiving support to extending it

Neetu was supported from Class 9 through M.Com during a period of profound financial difficulty after the loss of her father. She subsequently established a stable career with the State Bank of India.

Her continuing financial contribution to the Trust now helps another student move towards education and independence. Her journey demonstrates the multiplier effect in its simplest and most human form: care received, a life strengthened, and care offered again.
