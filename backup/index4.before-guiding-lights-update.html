The Beginning

A Flame Lit by Divine Purpose

Under a special mission, Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari and Lord Shiva brought their beloved son, Shri Nandkishore Ji Sharda — lovingly called Bhaiyaji — into this world, to give this age a path back to itself: simple, universal, and open to every seeker regardless of age, class, caste or religion.

Shri Nandkishore Sharda

Confronted by the stark reality of mortality at his father's pyre in 1959, then 15 year old Nandkishore Sharda experienced a profound awakening, turning away from material pursuits to decode the ultimate mystery of human existence. Driven by an unshakable determination to seek the eternal force behind life, he embarked on years of intense Sadhana and transformed into a profound spiritual master. His realizations ultimately culminated in the creation of Buddhi-Vivek Yog Sadhna, a universal practice through which his experiences and learnings continue to guide the masses today.

Basanti Ji Manihar

Beginning her spiritual journey in 1964 under the guidance of her maternal cousin, Bhaiya Shri Nandkishore Sharda, Maa Basanti Manihar embraced the spiritual path after undergoing rigorous examinations by her father to prove her dedication.

Following Bhaiyaji’s passing in 2003, she dedicated herself entirely to expanding his foundational vision into a flourishing legacy. Driven by unwavering resolve, she worked tirelessly through every adversity to perform selfless service (satkarma) and spread Jagatjanani Maa’s mission. She faithfully carried forward Bhaiyaji’s simplified divine knowledge for the welfare of the masses until her heavenly demise in 2021

Together, this quest for truth found a name to carry it forward.

Current President · Leading Since 2021

Madhubala Ji Advani

On December 9, 1970, a chance encounter in a hospital fundamentally transformed the life of 19-year-old Madhu Maa when she met Maa Basanti Manihar, who introduced her to Shri Nandkishore Ji Sharda (Bhaiyaji). Imbibing core values of empathy, equality, and dignity, Bhaiyaji became her spiritual guide alongside Maa Basanti, profoundly shaping her inner journey and spiritual evolution. Embracing her calling, Madhu Maa served as a selfless, dedicated grassroots worker alongside both mentors, actively visiting schools to advocate value-based education while quietly managing critical behind-the-scenes coordination without seeking recognition.

Following Bhaiyaji’s demise, Madhu Maa remained a steadfast pillar of the mission, preserving the continuity and smooth functioning of their shared spiritual vision.

Her dedication culminated in 2021 when, upon Maa Basanti Ji’s heavenly passing, she humbly stepped into the role of President to guide the Mission with efficiency, grace, and spiritual clarity. Under her steady leadership today, a team of qualified, selfless professionals from diverse fields oversees all activities, offering lifelong mentorship and expanding the reach of the Mission's sacred initiatives.

The Name

Why We Call It a Mission

When Madhubala Ji named this work a "Mission", it was not an institutional choice — it was an act of devotion, a tribute to the her spiritual guider who first lit this flame for her. The name "Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda Mission" has carried that meaning ever since.

Every activity that follows — scholarships, ration support, yoga, a sewing centre — is not a scattering of good intentions. Each was planned, in its season, toward one deliberate vision: the holistic development of every life the Mission touches.

"Selfless service is the highest expression of spiritual realization." — Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Sharda

Compassion in Action

How Compassion Became Action

Each initiative began by listening closely to a real need. Follow the journey, then explore the work behind every milestone.

It began with a conviction, shared by Bhaiyaji, Basanti Manihar and Madhubala Advani: that educating a girl does not change one life — it changes a family, and in time, a society.

10 June 1996

Swami Vivekanand Students' Welfare Charitable Trust

Established so that no deserving girl would be denied school education for want of money, beginning with financial support for girls from disadvantaged families.

Know more

As the scholarship programme found its footing, a new question followed the girls it had already helped: what happens when school ends, and college begins?

2001

Maa Shardamani Trust

Created to extend financial and academic assistance into college and higher education, so a young woman's ambition would not have to end where school did.

Know more

While walking alongside these families, the trust noticed something it could not unsee: sons in the very same households were also leaving school early, for the very same reason.

2010

Gyanyogi Shri Nandkishore Sharda Adhyatma Kendra

Our Guiding Principle

"We do not select for merit. We select for need — and then we build the person, not just the marksheet." Through weekly Sunday sessions, values, and steady moral support, quiet transformation follows: in discipline, in confidence, in how a young person carries themselves in the world.

Then came 2020. A survey of the students' own families surfaced three quiet crises: a widowed mother raising children alone, a father too ill to earn, a single income stretched over rent, school and food. The scholarship families were not just beneficiaries — they were family.

October 2020

Ration and Family Support

Begun for 50 families during the pandemic and designed to preserve dignity, the initiative now provides structured food assistance to roughly 300 families.

Know more

Underlying all of it, one belief held steady: a person grows whole only when four dimensions grow together — the physical, the mental, the spiritual, and the emotional.
