THE MISSION

Guided by Wisdom. Dedicated to Humanity.

The Mission is devoted to nurturing holistic human development through spiritual wisdom, compassionate service, and universal brotherhood.

Our Vision

To inspire individuals to live with wisdom, compassion, discipline and responsibility for the welfare of society.

Our Purpose

To promote spiritual awakening, selfless service and harmonious living through practical values and collective effort.

Our Story

A Journey That Began With Divine Purpose

Under a special mission, Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari brought her beloved son, Shri Nandkishore Ji Sharda (Bhaiyaji), to give the world a simple, universal spiritual path for today's age.

Sushri Basanti Ji Manihar was among the first to walk this path beside him, dedicating her life to nurturing it with compassion and discipline. Sushri Madhubala Ji Advani joined soon after, imbibing the same values of empathy and selfless service.

Together, their devotion gave rise to the Manidweep Adhyatma Parivar — a spiritual family united in one purpose: guiding humanity from the material toward the divine.

The Practice

Buddhi-Vivek Yog Sadhna

A simple, science-based path of wisdom and discernment, given by Bhaiyaji so that anyone — regardless of age, class or belief — can live fully in the world without being ruled by it.

ज्ञान

Why it matters today

In a fast-paced, materialistic age, Buddhi-Vivek offers a practical way to cultivate noble virtues while still meeting every worldly responsibility.

विवेक

What it brings together

It harmonizes wisdom, devotion, unselfish action and disciplined practice into one accessible, everyday method — not renunciation, but balance.

कर्म

What a practitioner gains

Joy, peace and inner steadiness — and a gradual, lived progression from humanity toward divinity.

Our Name, Our Philosophy

What Is Manidweep

The name Manidweep was given by Madhubala Ji Advani — a gesture of reverence toward her spiritual guide, Shri Nandkishore Sharda, whose wisdom continues to shape every initiative carried out in his name.

Every activity of the Mission follows a deliberate, long-term vision — never random, never fragmented. As society's needs have changed, new programmes have been introduced, but the underlying purpose has stayed constant: the holistic development of every individual — education, health, values, spirituality and social responsibility, woven into one balanced whole.

Compassion in Action

A Growing Journey of Service

What began with the education of deserving girls grew, year by year, into a wider movement for education, well-being and self-reliance.

1996

Swami Vivekanand Students' Welfare Charitable Trust

Founded in Jodhpur by Kumari Basanti Manihar, with Shri Nandkishore Sharda as President, so that no deserving girl would be denied school education for want of funds.

2001

Maa Shardamani Trust

Established as the scholarship programme matured, extending support beyond school into college and higher education for young women.

2010

Gyanyogi Shri Nandkishore Sharda Adhyatma Kendra

Created to support boys from economically disadvantaged families, later expanding into mentorship for Engineering, Medicine, Pharmacy, MBA and CA aspirants.

2020

Ration and Family Support

Begun during the pandemic with 50 families, the initiative grew into structured, dignity-preserving food assistance that now supports approximately 300 families.

2022

Yoga, Pranayama and Meditation

Introduced alongside academic support to nurture the physical, mental, emotional and spiritual dimensions of a well-rounded personality.

2025

Vatsalyamayi Maa Basanti Ji Manihar Sewing Centre

Founded to empower women and girls from underprivileged backgrounds through free training in sewing, tailoring and garment construction.
