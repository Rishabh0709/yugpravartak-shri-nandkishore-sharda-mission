"
Shri Nandkishore Sharda, Maa Basanti Manihar and Kumari Madubala Advani firmly believed that educating a girl empowers not only the individual but also her family and society at large. Guided by this conviction and inspired by the divine blessings of Jagatjanani Maa Tripursundari, Kumari Basanti Manihar established the Swami Vivekanand Students Welfare Charitable Trust in Jodhpur on 10 June 1996, with Shri Nandkishore Sharda serving as its President. The Trust was founded with the singular objective of ensuring that no deserving girl is deprived of education because of financial hardship.
Having imbibed the core values of empathy, equality, and dignity under the guidance of Shri Nandkishore Sharda and Maa Basanti Manihar, Kumari Madhubala Advani joined the trust at its inception as a selfless dedicated grassroots worker under Shri Sharda’s leadership.

The Trust has grown steadily through a systematic and phased approach, guided by clearly defined priorities and a long-term vision of holistic social development. During its formative years, when resources were limited, its foremost priority was to provide financial assistance to girls from economically disadvantaged families studying in government schools, enabling them to pursue their education with dignity.
As this scholarship programme became firmly established and sustainable, the Trust recognized the need to support girls beyond school education. Accordingly, the Maa Shardamani Trust was established in 2001 to provide financial and academic assistance to economically disadvantaged young women pursuing college and higher education, enabling them to realize their academic aspirations and professional potential.
While implementing these initiatives, the organization observed that many boys from the same economically disadvantaged families were also being forced to discontinue their education due to financial constraints. To address this unmet need, the Gyanyogi Shri Nandkishore Sharda Adhyatma Kendra was established in 2010. Initially focused on supporting economically disadvantaged male students, the Centre subsequently expanded its activities to include financial assistance, academic guidance, and mentoring for students pursuing Engineering, Medicine, Pharmacy, MBA, Chartered Accountancy, and other professional and technical courses.
The foundation of the organization's scholarship program is need-based assistance. Financial support is provided to students based on their economic circumstances and genuine need.
At the time of selection, a student's academic performance may be average. However, the organization's objective is not merely to provide financial aid but to foster the student's overall personality development.
Experience has shown that, through continuous guidance, value-based education, and moral support provided by the organization, students gradually demonstrate significant improvement in both their academic performance and overall merit. Many have gone on to achieve remarkable success in diverse fields of life.

Thus, the trust progressively broadened its mission—from supporting girls' school education to promoting higher and professional education for deserving students from economically weaker sections—creating a comprehensive and inclusive educational support system that enables talent to flourish irrespective of financial circumstances.
Throughout its journey, the trust's guiding principle has remained unwavering: no deserving student should be deprived of education because of economic hardship. As its resources and outreach expanded over the years, so too did the breadth of its educational initiatives, allowing it to serve an ever-growing number of deserving students while remaining true to its founding vision of education as a catalyst for lasting social transformation.
Through weekly Sunday knowledge sessions, continuous value-based education, and character-building activities conducted by Maa Basanti Manihar, Kumari Madhubala Advani and other members of the trust, students develop positive thinking, discipline, spiritual awareness, and a strong sense of social responsibility. As a result, they not only become responsible citizens but also serve as role models and sources of inspiration within society.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, the trust demonstrated that its scholarship recipients were not merely beneficiaries but valued members of its extended family. With this spirit, a comprehensive survey of the students' families was conducted to understand their actual needs and challenges.
The survey identified three major categories of families:
1.	Families in which the father had passed away, leaving an uneducated mother to single-handedly care for and support young children. 
2.	Families in which the father was seriously ill and there was no stable source of income. 
3.	Families that depended on a single earning member whose limited income was insufficient to meet the costs of rent, education, and basic household expenses. 
The pandemic was an exceptionally difficult period, particularly for economically disadvantaged families. Many lost their jobs during the lockdowns, causing their income to disappear almost entirely and pushing them into severe financial hardship.
These circumstances further strengthened the sense of compassion and social responsibility among the organization's volunteers and supporters toward underprivileged and economically vulnerable families. With this perspective, the trust did not view food distribution simply as an act of charity or philanthropy. Instead, it developed it into a structured support system that provided essential food supplies while preserving the dignity, self-respect, and self-esteem of the families receiving assistance.
This initiative began in October 2020 with support for 50 families. Through the continued generosity of society, the dedicated efforts of the organization, and the growing needs of the community, the program expanded steadily over time. Today, it provides regular food assistance to approximately 300 families, making a significant contribution to their daily livelihood and well-being.
The program was designed primarily to support the students associated with the organization and their families, helping them overcome difficult circumstances with dignity. As the financial condition of some families improved over time, they voluntarily withdrew from the program so that other families facing greater hardship could receive the benefit.

The trust believes that personality development extends far beyond formal education. A well-rounded personality requires the balanced development of four essential dimensions: physical, mental, spiritual, and emotional well-being.
When these four dimensions develop harmoniously, an individual's capabilities increase significantly, enabling them to pursue their goals more effectively while upholding strong ethical values.
With this vision, the trust introduced training in Yoga, Pranayama, and Meditation. These practices are not viewed merely as physical fitness programs; rather, they are intended to cultivate mental balance, self-discipline, positive thinking, inner peace, and emotional resilience.
A healthy body, a balanced mind, a spiritual outlook, and emotional maturity together empower individuals to face life's challenges with greater confidence, wisdom, and effectiveness.
With the objective of empowering women and young girls from economically disadvantaged, socially marginalized, and underprivileged sections of society, the Vatsalyamayi Maa Basanti Ji Manihar Sewing Centre was established in 2025.
The Centre provides free practical training in sewing, tailoring, and garment construction, enabling participants to develop marketable skills and become self-reliant through self-employment. Along with vocational training, the Centre also focuses on nurturing moral values, self-confidence, self-respect, and a positive outlook on life, empowering women to earn a dignified livelihood and lead independent lives.
