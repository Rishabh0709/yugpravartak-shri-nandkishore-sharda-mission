HOW IT ALL BEGAN

A Journey of Divine Guidance, Service and Continuity

The Mission emerged through divine purpose, was nurtured through selfless devotion, and continues today through steadfast leadership and service.

THE BEGINNING

Yugpravartak Shri Nandkishore Ji Sharda (Bhaiyaji)

Under a special mission, Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari and Lord Shiva (Babuji) brought their beloved son, the pioneer of a new age, Shri Nandkishore Ji Sharda (Bhaiyaji), to guide humanity in the modern age.

Through devotion, disciplined Sadhana and Maa’s grace, Bhaiyaji attained divine wisdom and opened a simple, universal path for living with virtue, detachment and inner peace: Buddhi-Vivek Yoga Sadhana, rooted in wisdom, discernment and inner transformation.

A GUIDING FORCE

Kumari Basanti Ji Manihar

Kumari Basanti Ji Manihar was among Bhaiyaji’s closest and most devoted companions in the Mission.

Guided by Bhaiyaji and blessed by Jagatjanani Maa Tripurasundari, she dedicated her life to nurturing the Mission with compassion, discipline and selfless service.

Her contribution helped shape the Manidweep Adhyatma Parivar, lovingly known as the Maa Parivar, and continues to inspire its spirit of humility, guidance and inner transformation.

CONTINUING THE MISSION

Madhubala Ji Advani

Having imbibed the core values of empathy, equality and dignity, Madhubala Ji joined the trust at its inception as a dedicated grassroots worker.

From visiting schools alongside Bhaiyaji and assessing student needs with compassion to meticulously managing scholarships behind the scenes, she threw herself wholeheartedly into every effort.

She also spearheaded weekly Sunday motivational sessions for young women, cultivating their character, self-confidence and sense of purpose.

Following the heavenly departure of Maa Basanti Ji in 2021, she humbly assumed responsibility as President, guiding the Mission’s initiatives with remarkable efficiency, grace and spiritual clarity.

Under her steadfast leadership, a dedicated team of highly qualified, selfless professionals from diverse fields seamlessly oversees all activities, providing lifelong mentorship and support across every endeavour.

COMPASSION IN ACTION

A Growing Journey of Service

What began with the education of deserving girls gradually grew into a wider movement supporting education, families, well-being and women’s empowerment.

Guided by the long-term vision of Shri Nandkishore Sharda, Maa Basanti Manihar, and Kumari Madhubala Advani, every initiative of the Trust is part of a thoughtful and evolving journey of service. Though its programmes have expanded over time to meet changing social needs, their purpose has remained constant: to help individuals lead balanced, value-oriented, self-reliant, compassionate, and fulfilling lives.

Rather than focusing on a single area, the Trust follows a holistic and inclusive approach—bringing together education, healthcare, human values, yoga and meditation, spiritual growth, women’s empowerment, environmental awareness, and social responsibility. Its work goes beyond providing support; it seeks to nurture the complete development of every individual.

1996

Swami Vivekanand Students’ Welfare Charitable Trust

Founded in Jodhpur by Kumari Basanti Manihar, with Shri Nandkishore Sharda as President, so that no deserving girl would be denied school education for want of funds.

2001

Maa Shardamani Trust

Established as the scholarship programme matured, extending support beyond school into college and higher education for young women.

2010

Gyanyogi Shri Nandkishore Sharda Adhyatma Kendra

Created to support boys from economically disadvantaged families, later expanding into mentorship for Engineering, Medicine, Pharmacy, MBA and CA aspirants.

2020

Ration and Family Support

Begun during the pandemic with 50 families, the initiative grew into structured, dignity-preserving food assistance that now supports approximately 300 families.

2022

Yoga, Pranayama and Meditation

Introduced alongside academic support to nurture the physical, mental, emotional and spiritual dimensions of a well-rounded personality.

2025

Vatsalyamayi Maa Basanti Ji Manihar Sewing Centre

Founded to empower women and girls from underprivileged backgrounds through free training in sewing, tailoring and garment construction.
